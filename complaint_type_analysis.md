# Complaint Type Analysis

The most abundant complaint type in January–February 2024 was **HEAT/HOT WATER**, with 81,641 complaints. The Bronx had the most (30,139), followed by Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens and Staten Island.

In June–July 2024, the same complaint type dropped to just 6,890, about 92% fewer. The drop happened in every borough, and the Bronx still had the most.

This shows that heat/hot water complaints are highly seasonal: they spike in winter, when buildings need heating, and mostly disappear in summer.
